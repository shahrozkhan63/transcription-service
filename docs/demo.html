Resilience

One bad chunk ≠ a failed job

  • Chunk-level isolation. Each piece transcribes independently — a transient failure retries just that chunk.
  • Jobs keep state. Error is captured, not swallowed — a failed job stays queryable and re-runnable.
  • Fail fast at the door. ffprobe rejects broken/empty files up front — never retried forever.
  • Production: queue retries w/ backoff + a dead-letter queue → nothing lost silently.

Beyond the code

The right design is cheap to run & safe to operate

Infrastructure

API and workers scale on different axes — don't pay for GPUs to parse HTTP. Autoscale on queue depth, not CPU.

Operations

Retention is a policy: encrypt, auto-delete audio, signed URLs. Retryable vs poison errors defined → the runbook is clear.

Support

Every result carries model, rtf & per-segment confidence — a dispute is answerable with data, not a shrug.

Business

Duration transcribed is the billing unit. Build-vs-buy is a real number: spiky volume → scale-to-zero; steady high volume → warm pool.

Serves UAE/GCC users → data residency & "right to delete" are constraints, not footnotes.

Normalize-everything · silence-aware chunking with timestamp offsets · async jobs · scale-to-zero · delete-the-audio — every decision serves one of those three.
